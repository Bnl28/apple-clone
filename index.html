04 — THE FINER DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is BNL One?

BNL One is an independent, fictional phone concept created for the BNL Studio web design portfolio. The branding, layouts, copy, and CSS-generated product artwork are original.

Can I buy this phone?

No. This is an interactive design showcase, not a shop. The configurator lets you explore colours and storage combinations, but no physical device, checkout, or reservation is offered.

Is this affiliated with a phone manufacturer?

No. BNL Studio is an independent creative concept and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple or any other phone manufacturer.

How was the artwork made?

The devices, lenses, materials, and abstract display graphics are built with CSS shapes and gradients. No third-party product photography or original-project assets are used.
